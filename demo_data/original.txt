Marine biology research highlights the incredible diversity of deep-sea hydrothermal vent ecosystems.
Chemosynthetic bacteria form the foundational tier of the food web in lightless abyssal depths.
Giant tube worms and blind shrimp thrive near superheated mineral-rich fluid vents.
Oceanographers continue to discover previously unclassified organism species in deep marine trenches.
Protecting benthic marine habitats requires international conservation agreements and regulated mining controls.
